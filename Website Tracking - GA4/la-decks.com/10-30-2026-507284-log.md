## Proposed approach (10-30, not built yet)
- After submit, the form redirects the top window to `/thanks/?submitted=1&lead_type=deck_build` (user confirmed). /thanks/ loads GTM-M4VDZBF8.
- leadtracker-6.js public API: `LeadTracker($).setGa4Options({sendTo,dimensionKey}).sendDataWithUrl($form, cb, url)` takes a jQuery `<form>`, sends GA4 `ss_lt_form_submission` with `form_data` = JSON of the field pairs, and POSTs formdata/url/referrer/visits to leadtracker.smartsites.com/leadtracker.php.
- Plan: 2 Custom HTML tags.
  1. `SS - LeadTracker Capture (LeadConnector)`: on /decks-builders/, listens for `set-sticky-contacts` from origin *.leadconnectorhq.com and saves the payload + LP URL in sessionStorage.
  2. `SS - LeadTracker Send (LeadConnector)`: on /thanks/ with `submitted=1`, reads and deletes the sessionStorage item, builds an in-memory hidden `<form>` from the fields (skipping ids), loads jquery-local + leadtracker-6.js and calls `sendDataWithUrl` with the LP URL.
- Why send on /thanks/: the redirect can cut off a hit sent from the LP. `_ud` in localStorage proves the parent page gets the message before leaving. sessionStorage is per tab, so stale or old leads aren't re-sent.

Common Plumbing Issues

  • Water Heater Troubles: High hot water demand strains water heaters. Sediment buildup or thermostat issues can lead to insufficient hot water or leaks.
  • Sewer Line Blockages: Tree roots and shifting soil can block sewer lines, causing backups and damage.
  • Leaky Fixtures: San Diego's hard water can lead to leaks in faucets and fixtures. Address them promptly to save water and prevent damage.
  • Clogged Drains: Hair, grease, soap scum, and food scraps are the most common culprits behind slow or blocked drains in San Diego homes. Kitchen sinks and shower drains are the most frequent trouble spots.

- Booking goal = `oaiq("measure","order_created",{type:"contents",amount:<int cents>,currency:"USD"},{event_id})`.
- `debug:true` should be removed before go-live.
- Cookies `__oppref`/`__obref` are per domain, so the base tag must load on both www and booking.
- CSP needs bzrcdn.openai.com and bzr.openai.com if the site sends a CSP header.

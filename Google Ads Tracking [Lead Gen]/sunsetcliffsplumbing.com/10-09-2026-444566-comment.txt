Re: I can still see we are getting conversions from this number (SS: Calls From Website # (858) 299-2878)
....
This conversion action is still active in GTM and loads on every page. For ad visitors, Google replaces the (858) 299-2878 number with its own forwarding number wherever it still shows on the page (see point 2 above), and calls to it are counted under this action. Because both Google's number replacement and our PPC number swap act on the same numbers when the page loads, whichever runs first wins. So some PPC calls can be counted under the (858) 299-2878 or (619) 416-7071 action instead of "SS: Calls From Website #(619-786-2032)". This is the most likely reason the PPC call action shows 0.

Re: Clicks to call / Call Button Click are counting calls but they are not showing under calls
....
These two actions count clicks on a phone number, not actual phone calls, so they are grouped under Contacts and not under Phone call leads. A click does not always become a call. "Calls From Website" actions only count completed calls made to the Google forwarding number, so the numbers will not match.

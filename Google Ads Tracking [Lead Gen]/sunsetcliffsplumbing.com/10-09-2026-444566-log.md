### Findings
1. **Replacer priority bug** — `conditional-replacer-3.js` checks all conditions in "cookie" mode first, then "referrer". Constructor writes `referrer_site` cookie from document.referrer before run. Ad click (referrer google.com) + `?id=ppc` on first page: BY_PARAMS cookie not set yet → SEARCH_ENGINE cookie matches → shows **619-786-2031**. Next pages show 2032. Direct `?id=ppc` (how FED tested) shows 2032. Simulated in node (scratchpad sim.js): direct → 2032; google ref → 2031; next page → 2032; organic → 2031. Shared LeadTracker lib — may affect other clients too.
2. **Not swapped (outside rootSelector)** across 79 sitemap pages: `sms:858-299-2878` links (service/location/blog pages), paragraph text (619) 416-7071 (Del Mar, Point Loma, Rancho Santa Fe, Coronado + "-plumber" variants, privacy, terms), "contact us at 858-299-2878" paragraphs (service pages), homepage `<a>619-874-7900</a>` (no href).

4. **Race**: Google awcc tags for 858/416 numbers (gtm.js) vs async replacer chain (jquery-local → conditional-replacer). Whichever first wins → PPC calls can land in 858/416 actions. Not verified in a browser.

What this means for comment #49

Untracked calls

/heat-pump/ uses a different number

Five buttons dial (781) 819-2201. Google Ads does not count those calls. Two fixes:

  • Change the page to (781) 952-9012, or
  • Create a new call conversion for 819-2201.

First confirm the page is still used in a campaign.

Email uptick

A floating email button on every page

The email address shows about three times per page, including a button that follows the visitor down the page. Each click is counted as Email Contact. That probably explains the rise more than a form error does. The forms still need a test.

To confirm

Location page form IDs

If the location pages use a form other than 170, their submissions reach GA4 but never Google Ads. This is the biggest open question.

How the form reports a submit (confirmed 09-30 in Tag Assistant)
=================================================================
On the thank-you page load DealerOn pushes (right after do.init):
  dataLayer.push({
    event: "do.lead_submitted",
    do: {
      form_name: "Website - Contact Us",
      form_type: "consumer_contact",
      product_name: "Website|Contact Us",
      page_type: "contact",
      submission_id: 58010003,      <- same as lid= in the URL
      ...
    }
  })
DealerOn stores the lead in sessionStorage on submit and pushes it on thankyou.aspx,
so the pixel fires on a normal page load (not lost during the redirect).

  Object Properties:     content_name = form submit best price

  Event ID:              {{SS - DLV - do.submission_id}}
  Triggering:            SS - CE - Lead - Contact Us

IMPORTANT - avoid double counting:
  If the all-forms tag "SS - FB - Lead - Form submit" (trigger SS - CE - do.lead_submitted, no filter) also exists,
  Contact Us would fire Lead twice. Pick ONE:
  a) Keep only the all-forms tag (content_name = {{SS - DLV - do.form_name}}), and skip Steps 4-5, OR
  b) Use per-form tags like this one for every form, and delete/pause the all-forms tag.
  Also don't add a thankyou.aspx Page View Lead tag on top of this.

Below are the steps for the client and us to connect social profiles to GSC.

Steps to Connect Accounts from a personal Google profile.

  - Open Google Search Console and log in.
  - Click the property drop-down menu in the top-left corner.
  - Select + Add property.
  - Choose the desired social network (Instagram, TikTok, X, or YouTube) from the platform property section.
  - Sign in to the social account via the OAuth pop-up and click Allow to authorize read-only access.
 
Steps to Connect Accounts from agencies.

  - Open separate tabs in the browser.
  - Log out of any personal accounts on Instagram, TikTok, or X.
  - Log in to the first specific business or client profile you need to add.
  - Navigate to the Google Search Console dashboard.
  - Click the Property Selector drop-down menu in the top-left corner.
  - Scroll to the bottom and click + Add property.
  - Choose the platform (Instagram, TikTok, X, or YouTube) under the Platform Property section.
  - Authorize and Repeat
  - Click Add next to the platform.

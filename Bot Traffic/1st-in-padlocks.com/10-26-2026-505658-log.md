## GA4-side options
- GA4 data filters only support internal/developer traffic by IP; no country/device filter. Bot IPs rotate. Filters are never retroactive. → not viable.
- Recorded data can't be deleted from GA4.
- Going forward via GTM (exception trigger on screen resolution) only partly works: G-N6S24B8XH5 is sent both by the Shopify Google & YouTube app web pixel AND by GTM-5XNKGSP (also G-RKC2JS70QB in GTM). The app pixel can't be conditioned. Possible duplicate GA4 tagging — flag separately, not in scope.
- Shopify: no edge country blocking available.

## Recommendation
Looker Studio filter: Exclude where Screen resolution IN (1366x1366, 1280x1200) — instead of the country filter. Removes all three waves (Jun/Jul/Sep), keeps real SG/CN shoppers, fixes June. Same exclusion as a GA4 segment/comparison for ad-hoc reports.

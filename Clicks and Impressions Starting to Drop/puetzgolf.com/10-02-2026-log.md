## BED findings (comment #7)
Live product pages are not blocked by a firewall. The 403 comes at the end of a redirect chain:
1. A nonexistent URL (product disabled/deleted, or URL key changed) returns a 404 in Magento.
2. Amasty Advanced Search turns the 404 into a 302 to `/search/<words>?amnoroute`.
   Setting: Stores > Configuration > Amasty Extensions > Advanced Search > General > "Enable Redirect from 404 to Search Results" = Yes.
3. `/search/` and `/catalogsearch/` return a 403 to bots.
4. GSC records that 403 against the original URL.

- robots.txt has an empty `User-agent: Googlebot` group with an empty `Disallow:`, so Googlebot ignores `Disallow: /search/`. This fix is from task #94 and is still not deployed.
- Sample mix: about 86% of impressions are discontinued products. About 12% are **active products whose URL changed with no 301**, and that traffic can be recovered:
  - `/qi35-driver-qi35-driver` → `/taylormade-qi35-driver`
  - `/melin-compass-hydro-hat` → `/melin-hydro-compass-melin-hydro-compass`
- The 09-05 jump (11.7K → 20.6K) was a GSC reprocessing batch. Indexed and Not indexed grew together, so no new blocking rule appeared.
- Verify: Rich Results Test on `/search/golf` → "Blocked due to access forbidden (403)". An active product returns 200.

## Correction to 09-29 note
The 09-29 note pointed at Cloudflare. The `cf-mitigated: challenge` we saw was the browser and bot challenge for our own test requests. The 403 Googlebot gets comes from the `/search/` redirect, not from a firewall on product pages. The `/search/` 403 rule itself can stay.
